03 / PROBLEM DEFINITION

Problem & Objectives

The central problem is how to make relevant agricultural decision support more accessible to Sri Lankan smallholder farmers using data-driven methods.

Main objective

To design and develop an integrated Smart Agriculture Support System that applies AI and IoT concepts to support crop selection, soil-health understanding, nutrient and fertiliser decisions, and agricultural market intelligence.

Specific objectives

  • Develop a crop recommendation and cultivation-guidance component using location and seasonal context.
  • Explore soil-health monitoring using sensor-oriented data and intelligent analysis.
  • Build a nutrient prediction and fertiliser-support component to inform crop nutrient decisions.
  • Provide market-related insights that can support farmers' planning and selling decisions.
  • Integrate the components into a coherent system and evaluate them against suitable criteria.
