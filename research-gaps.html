02 / IDENTIFIED NEEDS

Research Gaps

Smallholder farmers may need to make crop, soil, fertiliser and selling decisions while agricultural information is spread across different sources or difficult to interpret.

Gaps this research addresses

  • Fragmented support: crop planning, soil information, nutrient decisions and market insights are often considered separately.
  • Context sensitivity: recommendations need to reflect local conditions such as district, season and crop choice.
  • Information accessibility: technical outputs should be presented in a way that is useful to non-specialist users.
  • Practical decision support: farmers need actionable guidance, not only raw measurements or model predictions.

Research opportunity

A modular AI- and IoT-enabled platform can bring these information streams into one decision-support experience, while each component focuses on a specific agricultural problem.

Align these research gaps with final literature evidence. Do not present them as proven findings until supported by sources.
