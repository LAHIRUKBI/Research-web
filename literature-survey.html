Literature Survey

Agricultural decision-support research increasingly explores machine learning, sensor technologies and data analytics to improve farm-level decisions. This project brings those approaches together around the practical context of Sri Lankan smallholder agriculture.

Areas reviewed

  • Machine-learning approaches for crop recommendation using environmental, seasonal and location-related factors.
  • Soil monitoring approaches using sensor data and intelligent interpretation of soil conditions.
  • Nutrient assessment and fertiliser recommendation methods that support responsible input use.
  • Agricultural market information systems and data-driven approaches to understanding price trends.

Research direction
