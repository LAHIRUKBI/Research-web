04 / SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

The 4 System Components

Each component focuses on a connected agricultural decision. Together, they form the proposed Smart Agriculture Support System.

01 · Crop Recommendation

Uses location and target month/season to recommend suitable crops and provide cultivation guidance. The current component work includes a Random Forest model.

02 · Soil Nutrient & Fertiliser Recommendation

Uses fertiliser and crop-related information to estimate soil N-P-K conditions and support fertiliser decisions.

03 · Soil Health Monitoring

Explores the combination of IoT/sensor observations and AI-based analysis to help interpret soil-health conditions.

04 · Agricultural Market Intelligence

Provides market-focused insights, including price-related analysis, to support more informed agricultural planning.
