Core Capabilities

One Connected Agriculture Ecosystem

SmartAgri unites four focused research modules into a unified decision-support platform, addressing the end-to-end needs of Sri Lankan farming communities.

🌱
COMPONENT 01

Crop Recommendation & Cultivation Guidance

Machine learning model predicting suitable crops based on district weather, monsoon seasonality (Yala/Maha), and soil attributes, paired with actionable cultivation guidance.

Explore Module ↗
🧪
COMPONENT 02

Soil Nutrient & Fertiliser Recommendation

Predictive analytics estimating soil Nitrogen, Phosphorus, and Potassium (N-P-K) dynamics, delivering customized chemical fertiliser blend recipes to curb over-application.

Explore Module ↗
📡
COMPONENT 03

Soil Health Monitoring & Telemetry

Field-deployed IoT microcontroller sensor nodes continuously measuring volumetric soil moisture, temperature, and electrical conductivity to trigger early stress warnings.

Explore Module ↗
📈
COMPONENT 04

Agricultural Market Intelligence

Time-series price forecasting and demand trend analytics capturing wholesale pricing at major regional economic centers, assisting farmers with harvest scheduling.
